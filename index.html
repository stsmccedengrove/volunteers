NO PARKING Inside School Premises

Parking is strictly prohibited inside St. Bernadette High School premises. Private vehicles will not be granted access to the school driveway or grounds.

🅿️

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

721 Wells Road, Whitchurch, Bristol, BS14 9HU

*(Approx. 1 mile from school venue. Direct entry & exit on Wells Road)*

Coach & Bus Drop-off Guidelines

Do NOT drive coaches inside the school grounds as there is no turning space available. All coaches carrying participants must drop off passengers at New Fosseway Road. Passengers should walk via the designated footpath to the school entrance.
